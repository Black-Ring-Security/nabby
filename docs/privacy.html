Nabby Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-09

Nabby ("the app") is an SSH and SFTP client developed by Black Ring Security.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

We do not run any servers that receive your data.

Data stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following only on your device:

  • Host profiles (names, addresses, usernames, port forwards, settings)
  • Passwords, private keys and key passphrases you choose to save. These are

encrypted with the Android Keystore.

  • Known host key fingerprints

This data never leaves your device, except:

  • when you connect, the app sends your credentials **directly to the SSH server you

chose**, over an encrypted SSH connection;

  • when you export a backup yourself (backups never contain passwords or keys).

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes everything.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state: connect to your SSH servers.
  • Notifications and foreground service: show an ongoing notification that keeps

your SSH sessions connected while the app is in the background, and alerts when

a session disconnects.

  • Wake lock: keep sessions and the screen awake while you work, if enabled.
  • Files (system picker only): import keys/configs and upload or save files you pick.

Updates

App updates are delivered by Google Play. The app asks Google Play whether an update

is available; no personal data is sent by the app for this.

Children

The app is a technical tool for system administrators and is not directed at children.
